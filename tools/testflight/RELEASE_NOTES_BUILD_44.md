Build 44: Smarter drive review.

• Drive review: choose who drove (you or someone else) and which vehicle (one of yours, or another vehicle) as two separate choices, so a passenger in your own car, a rental or an Uber is recorded correctly
• Rentals: a recent rental or other vehicle is suggested again for your next drive
• Lock it in: confirming drives is much faster, including "Lock in all" in My Drives
• Trip maps: the whole route with its start and end is always visible and centred, on the details screen and the review sheet, and re-fits when you fold or rotate
• Odometer and distance totals follow the new vehicle choice
