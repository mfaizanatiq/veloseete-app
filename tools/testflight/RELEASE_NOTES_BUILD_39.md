Build 39: A smarter fuel gauge, rental drives, a cleaner Service tab, and a faster launch.

• Fuel: tank level now tracks litres actually in the tank — topping up early or a small fill-to-full no longer fakes an empty tank
• Odometer: ignores readings that run backwards, splits drives that straddle a fill, and stops one missed trip from skewing calibration
• Drives: review sheet picks the car — or "Rental / other" for a rental or a friend's car (counts as your driving, not your car's odometer)
• Trip detail: route story on the map with a from → to line, stat tiles, and a one-line pace summary
• Service: same layout as Fuels, a "Next up" card with km/days left, 18 common services in a searchable picker, and suggested reminders
• Speed: live drive screen and trip detail feel lighter; launch splash plays in full once per update, then about 1 s — tap to skip
• Notifications, widgets and the Live Activity open the app without the splash
