# Veloseete — Beta App Description (TestFlight)

Paste into **App Store Connect → TestFlight → Test Information → Beta App Description**.

---

Veloseete is a car co-pilot for everyday drivers. It automatically captures trips in the background, helps you log fuel fills, tracks efficiency against your car’s baseline, and keeps service history in one place — without spreadsheet energy.

This beta is pre-release software. We’re especially interested in feedback on:

- **Trip tracking** — auto-detect accuracy, Live Activity fuel bar, and confirming pending drives  
- **Onboarding** — sign-up, permissions, and starting without adding a car first  
- **Fuels & Driver** — fill logging, spend trends, and efficiency insights  
- **Garage** — adding a car, paint colors, and linking earlier drives  

Location, motion, and notifications are used for drive detection and local reminders. Data syncs to your account via Firebase when signed in.

Thank you for helping shape Veloseete before 1.0.
