Veloseete × Wheelz — UI similarity & differentiation report

What overlaps, what's actually risky, and where to take influence from instead — sourced from Mobbin.

Generated Jul 31, 2026 · Research: Mobbin iOS library · Constraint: keep all existing features, drawers, colors, and heatmap lines.

The short answer

You're unlikely to face a legal problem — dark map + heatmap + bottom drawer + trip list are generic industry patterns used by Strava, Slopes, Flighty, and dozens of others. Look-and-feel alone is weakly protected; trade-dress claims need distinctive, non-functional elements plus consumer confusion. The real risk is perception: reviewers, users, or Wheelz's team noticing a near-clone. Three things currently make Veloseete read as "Wheelz-like" and all three are cheap to change: the identical sort labels, the identical drive-row anatomy, and the drawer header structure. Fix those and the two apps share only what every map-based tracker shares.

Apply: group drives under day headers (Today / Yesterday / Jul 28), lead each row with distance in Space Grotesk instead of speed, and add per-day distance totals to headers. Route thumbnail moves inside a subtle inset on the right or is dropped from rows (it lives in the detail view). Speed becomes a small secondary metric — this alone kills the Wheelz row resemblance.
