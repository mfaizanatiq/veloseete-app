# Portfolio captions

Short lines you can paste under each shot.

## 01 · Fuels hero
**You vs the brochure, honestly.**  
Rolling L/100km against a seeded manufacturer baseline — with a gauge that makes “beating spec” obvious.

## 02 · Fill history
**A fuel ledger, not a form dump.**  
Lifetime fills, spend, and volume — grouped by month with running subtotals.

## 03 · Fill detail
**Every tank tells a story.**  
Cost, interval efficiency, distance since last fill, and the pump pinned on the map.

## 04 · Live drive
**Trip tracking that feels alive.**  
Full-bleed route, distance / time / speed HUD, one tap to end.

## 05 · Pending review
**Auto-capture with a human gate.**  
Motion + GPS propose the drive; you confirm or discard before it hits history.

## 06 · Driver insights
**Analytics with a personality.**  
All-time distance, witty insight cards, and achievement badges that unlock from real habits.

## 07 · Garage
**A personal fleet OS.**  
Multiple cars, active selection, tank capacity and odometer at a glance.

## 08 · Low-fuel notification
**Quiet until the tank is actually low.**  
Fill pattern × liters × efficiency × km driven × confidence — then one alert per fill cycle.

## 09 · Drive & service nudges
**Reminders that sound like Veloseete.**  
Pending-drive pokes and service countdowns with the same quirky voice.

## 10 · Fuel intelligence
**The math behind the nudge.**  
Range budget, km burned, confidence score, urgency — shown so the product feels trustworthy.

## 11 · Decision flow
**Five steps. No cry wolf.**  
Learn range → watch km → score confidence → classify urgency → cooldown.

## 12 · Station map
**Log the pump, not just the liters.**  
MapKit search for nearby stations at fill time.
