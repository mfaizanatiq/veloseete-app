Veloseete · Notifications

Alerts that wait until the tank is actually low

Multi-signal on-device logic: fill patterns, liters, efficiency, km driven, and a confidence gate — with a per-fill cooldown so it never spams.

  • No calendar-only “you usually fill on day 4” false alarms
  • Unreviewed GPS drives still count toward range
  • Quirky copy with precise km / % remaining
Veloseete · Drives

Auto-capture the drive. Let the driver confirm.

Background motion + GPS build a route. Pending review keeps noisy detections from polluting history — and still informs the fuel-range model.

  • Live HUD while recording
  • Confirm / discard with suggested odometer
  • Feeds low-fuel intelligence between fills
